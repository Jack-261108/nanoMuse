# nanoMuse 1.0.4 · Compass

🐉 **nanoMuse `1.0.4` “Compass”**: a health check of the whole project, a day after Helm; what nine passes over the relay, the runtime, the five clients, the showcase, the docs and the CI found is fixed here, and DeepSeek Harness joins the coding agents.

1.0.3 went out on 2026-10-12. The same day we read every part of nanoMuse against every other part and against what the apps do on a phone, an iPad and a computer: the relay, the Python runtime, the web console, the desktop, the Android and iPhone apps, the hosted showcase, the documentation in both languages and the workflows that build all of it. The findings fill this version. Some you will see at once: an iPad's chat is one centred column under a frosted pill bar instead of three different widths; a rename or a new face made on one device reaches the others without a restart; a step another device's Muse asks about is answered on the iPhone where the task was typed; a screen reader hears the web console's replies. Others you will not see and should not have to: an MCP server's secrets live in the vault rather than a plain file, the Android proxy password is encrypted, a signed-out device loses its line to the hub at once, the invite bonus is paid when the friend first uses nanoMuse Cloud rather than at sign-up, every operator action on the relay is on record, and every GitHub Action is pinned to a commit. Beside the check, @cutey-none added DeepSeek Harness as a fourth coding agent on the desktop ([#354](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/354)), @brainsparker proposed You.com as a web search that needs no key ([#360](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/issues/360)), @Ichiro786 blended the Android chat's navigation surround ([#397](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/397)), and @Mola-maker's reports and fixes on the Android app's speech input and tests are in ([#356](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/issues/356), [#394](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/394), [#405](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/405)). Nothing moves for a person upgrading: the Android package installs over 1.0.3 and keeps everything, the desktop keeps its settings, and a 1.0.3 client works against the relay (0.25.0) unchanged.

## Highlights

- **The iPad's chat is one column, and the pill bar is glass again.** On an iPad the messages, the composer and the pill bar share one centred column of 760 pt, the web console's width; the composer and the pill bar are exactly as wide as each other, and the agent's replies grow from a 340 pt pill to 600 pt. The conversation now scrolls under the composer and the pill bar on every iPhone and iPad, so the bar blurs the rows behind it as it blurs the Feed and the Library, and a fade of the canvas colour rises above the composer while older messages are being read; in 1.0.3 the bar sat on a plain canvas and read as a solid pill, and the last message was cut off at the composer's top edge. A side chat's header dissolves like the main chat's, Ideas, Goals and the Library stand on the chat's canvas, and Reduce Motion is honoured throughout: the pill bar's indicator moves without the spring, the face keeps still, the slides, the drawer and the bottom bar crossfade ([#406](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/406), [#411](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/411), [#423](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/423)).
- **Your devices agree with each other.** Signing in on an iPhone brings the account's chats, name and face down at once, instead of after a trip to the background; a rename or a connection made on a phone without a face of its own no longer puts the dragon over the account's face on every other device; the name and the face another device changed arrive when the app comes back to the front; a chat older than a week can be renamed again and the title follows; a fresh device joins the account's main chat on its first pull instead of starting a second one; and the model's hidden notes stay out of the synced chat. On Android a chat model picked under *Settings › Models* answers the main chat's first message too, a group picked in a chat's picker is the default for new chats with the group's thinking level, and signing in to another account starts from that account's own name and face ([#408](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/408), [#409](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/409), [#410](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/410), [#421](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/421)).
- **A step another device's Muse asks about is answered on the iPhone.** A task sent with `@〈device〉` runs on that device's Muse; when a step there needs a yes, the question used to be declined from the phone with a line telling you to approve it over there. The phone now shows the card where the task was typed, with the Android phone's four answers: *Allow once*, *Allow for this chat*, *Always allow this on 〈device〉* (listed with *Revoke* under *Approvals* on the agent's page) and *Deny*; a payment gets only once and deny, no answer within the time the other side allows counts as a no, and a question answered first at the other device closes the card ([#424](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/424)).
- **DeepSeek Harness is a fourth coding agent.** The *Coding agents* page on the desktop, and from the phone over the hub, knew Cursor, Codex and Claude Code; the chats of `dsh web` and DeepSeek's desktop app under `~/.dsh` are now listed beside them, a transcript is shown, and a message is sent into one with `dsh --profile headless --json`, through a `dsh` on `PATH` or else the harness the app ships. The store is zstd written one frame at a time, and every frame is read. Written by @cutey-none ([#354](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/354)); the Python runtime does not list dsh yet.
- **A web search that needs no key.** `provider = "you"` under `[connectors.search]`, or the You.com chip under *Connections → Web search*, searches You.com without an account, 100 times a day per address; past that the result says so and DuckDuckGo answers once. A key of your own moves the searches to the keyed API with higher limits, and the Sentinel shows whichever host is in use. DuckDuckGo stays the default. Proposed by @brainsparker ([#360](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/issues/360)).
- **Secrets where they belong.** An MCP server's `env` values typed in the app went into `app-settings.json` as plain text; each now goes into the encrypted vault as `MCP_<SERVER>_<KEY>` and the file keeps a reference, and the web console's MCP form has an *Environment* field that says so. An endpoint address with a password in it is refused, a key is no longer sent to a host that was not named when an endpoint redirects, and `nanomuse config show` masks every kind of credential. The Android proxy password moves to an encrypted file, the iPhone's to the keychain, a password saved by 1.0.3 is moved over on the first read. Creating a reminder, a trigger or a goal counts as a change, so `strict` mode asks first; the browser refuses the carrier-grade NAT range with the other private ranges; a calendar feed that fails no longer shows its private link ([#387](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/387), [#416](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/416), [#421](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/421), [#423](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/423)).
- **The relay keeps better books.** The invite bonus is paid when the invited friend first uses nanoMuse Cloud, not at sign-up, so padding a pool with throwaway accounts no longer pays; the admin page shows who invited whom, names the inviters worth a look and has one action for them; every operator action on an account is on record; a disabled account is told so before a code is sent; deleting an account forgets it at once; a device that is signed out loses its hub socket the same moment, so a lost phone cannot reach the account's other devices meanwhile; the web console open in two tabs no longer fights itself on the hub; `TRUSTED_PROXIES` narrows the per-address limits too; backups take a consistent copy and can be read by the deploying user ([#384](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/384), [#391](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/391), [#401](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/401), [#413](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/413)).
- **The web console reads aloud and takes the keyboard.** A screen reader hears that the agent is writing and reads the reply once it is complete; the menus open on their first entry, walk with the arrow keys and close on Escape; the browser viewer scrolls with the keys; the tool calling chips are translated and the chosen one is marked; the feed and a skill say when they could not load, with *Try again*; adding an MCP server with its tools marked *high* no longer fails, since the chip is *sensitive*, the level the runtime knows ([#382](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/382), [#414](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/414)).
- **Android, fixed where it broke.** A Claude sign-in no longer closes the app: the provider refuses the send with one sentence, and a build without the identifier does not offer the sign-in ([#373](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/issues/373), reported by @moh8919-dev). Remembering a payment approval no longer closes the app either, since the manifest now declares the permission the screen lock needs. System voice input picks the right service and language errors for its fallback ([#393](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/issues/393), fixed by @Mola-maker in [#394](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/394)), cancelling provider voice input closes the microphone at once ([#356](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/issues/356)), a clip of your own stops copying at 30 MB, quoted words show their quotes again, the hub no longer takes the app down when the socket drops mid-task, a task from another device is answered in that device's language, the pill bar reads as tabs under TalkBack, the chat's navigation surround blends into the rounded composer fade ([#397](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/397) by @Ichiro786), and `./gradlew :app:lintDebug` runs again.
- **iPhone, fixed where it broke.** The ••• menu's *New Chat* opens a chat again ([#392](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/issues/392), reported by @bldr3); a wrong password says so, in all nine languages; the nanoMuse Cloud row under Providers stops saying *0% of the allowance left*; the relay's key is written to this phone's keychain only, from the first moment; a clip of your own stays out of the backups and accepts every H.264 file; the routines copy says what the phone does; three translations put their numbers back in order.
- **The desktop keeps up.** A failed update check is tried again within the hour; a clip of your own may be HEVC where this computer can play it, the same rule as every other device; a fragmented MP4 is read right; a release gets the installers of every platform that built, and the workflows check that every hook the chat's stylesheet leans on exists in the dsh version being staged ([#383](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/383), [#415](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/415)).
- **The showcase and the project.** The hosted showcase believes `X-Forwarded-For` from Caddy alone, may be framed by the project's sites only, does not count a demo the box failed to start, speaks Chinese on its own pages, is built from one pinned MobileGym commit, carries health checks and says on the privacy page what it keeps ([#381](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/381), [#417](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/417)). Every GitHub Action is pinned to a commit, the desktop's checks run on Windows on every pull request, the release scripts refuse a half-done release, the issue forms read in English and Chinese, the docs match the code after an audit of both languages, and the READMEs' visitor counter runs on hits.sh ([#378](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/378), [#380](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/380), [#390](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/390), [#412](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/412), [#418](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/418)).
- **TestFlight.** Builds 18 (1.0.1) and 19 (1.0.2) passed Apple's beta review on 2026-10-12, so the public link installs build 19 today; build 22 (1.0.3) is in that review; 1.0.4 goes up as build 26 and is submitted to the same review. The link installs a build once its review has passed, the internal testers before that.

## Upgrade Notes

- **Android installs over 1.0.3 and keeps your data.** `nanoMuse-1.0.4-arm64.apk` (Android 8.0+, arm64; versionCode 47) is signed with the same key as every version before it. Verify with `sha256sum -c nanoMuse-1.0.4-arm64.apk.sha256`. The proxy password, if you set one, is moved into an encrypted file on the first read; nothing else moved.
- **iPhone and iPad come through TestFlight.** The public link installs build 19 (1.0.2), which passed Apple's beta review with build 18; build 22 (1.0.3) is still in that review, and build 26 (1.0.4) is submitted when it is uploaded; a build reaches the link once its review has passed, the internal testers before that. The proxy password, if you set one, moves to the keychain on the first read.
- **The desktop installers are built by CI from the tag `v1.0.4`.** The Mac packages are not notarized: the first time, open the app from *System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway* (or right-click → *Open*). The Windows package is not signed: click *More info* and *Run anyway* once. On Linux prefer the `.deb`; the AppImage needs FUSE (`--appimage-extract-and-run` without). They arrive a little after the APK, and `SHA256SUMS-desktop.txt` lists every checksum. Your settings stay where they were; a computer with dsh chats under `~/.dsh` shows them under *Settings → Coding agents*.
- **The relay is 0.25.0; a 1.0.3 client works against it unchanged.** For operators: the invite bonus is now paid at the invited account's first charged request, accounts invited before this rule are marked paid when the relay first starts with it, new accounts from an e-mail domain that is not a well-known mail provider are limited to `SIGNUP_PER_DOMAIN_DAY` per rolling day (20 by default, 0 turns it off; `SIGNUP_DOMAIN_ALLOW` adds your own domains), `TRUSTED_PROXIES` now also governs the per-address limits, `GET /v1/config` carries `code_ttl_s` and `password_min_len`, a revoked key closes its hub socket with `4001 bad_key`, and the backup copies are owned by the relay directory's owner (or `BACKUP_OWNER`), mode 600. [docs/self-hosting.md](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/blob/main/docs/self-hosting.md) and [cloud/README.md](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/blob/main/cloud/README.md) have the details.
- **The runtime's settings file is written whole or not at all.** `app-settings.json` is written beside the old one and renamed over it; an MCP server's `env` values are moved into the vault the next time the server is added from the app, and a plain value in `config.toml` still works. `config.toml` keeps every key it had; `[connectors.search] provider = "you"` is new and optional.

## Community

nanoMuse is open source, free and non-profit. Sign in with a phone number or an e-mail and you get a starting allowance; the developer pays for it. The account page shows what is left and how to add more. When it is gone, use your own key or a plan you already pay for ([how](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/blob/main/docs/own-key.md)); the key you add can take over any of the four jobs, and the account can be switched off as a model source while you keep it for sync and your devices. With an account, the text of your main conversation is kept on nanoMuse Cloud so that your devices show the same chat, side chats only if you say so, and one switch in Data controls turns it off and deletes it; nothing is sold ([privacy policy](https://nanomuse.cn/privacy/)); delete the account whenever you want. The same relay runs on a server of yours: [docs/self-hosting.md](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/blob/main/docs/self-hosting.md).

🐉 **nanoMuse `1.0.4`「Compass」**：Helm 之后一天，对整个项目做了一次体检；对中继、运行时、五个客户端、展示站、文档和 CI 的九遍检查查出来的问题都在这里修了，DeepSeek Harness 也加入了编程助手。

1.0.3 在 2026-10-12 发出。同一天我们把 nanoMuse 的每个部分对照着其他部分、对照着 App 在手机、iPad 和电脑上的实际表现读了一遍：中继、Python 运行时、网页控制台、桌面端、Android 和 iPhone 的 App、托管的展示站、两种语言的文档，以及把这一切构建出来的工作流。查出来的东西填满了这个版本。有些一眼就能看到：iPad 的聊天是一根居中的列、底下是毛玻璃底栏，而不是三种不同的宽度；在一台设备上改名或换脸，其他设备不用重启就能收到；别的设备上的 Muse 问起某一步时，在输入任务的那部 iPhone 上就能回答；读屏软件能听到网页控制台的回复。另一些你看不到，也本不该需要看到：MCP 服务器的密钥放进保险库而不是明文文件，Android 的代理密码加密保存，退出登录的设备立刻失去它到 hub 的连线，邀请奖励在朋友第一次使用 nanoMuse Cloud 时才发而不是注册时，中继上操作者的每一步都有记录，每个 GitHub Action 都钉到了具体提交。体检之外，@cutey-none 在桌面端加了 DeepSeek Harness 作为第四个编程助手（[#354](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/354)），@brainsparker 提议把不用 key 的 You.com 加进网页搜索（[#360](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/issues/360)），@Ichiro786 让 Android 聊天的导航区域和输入框的圆角渐变融在一起（[#397](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/397)），@Mola-maker 关于 Android 语音输入和测试的报告与修复也都合入了（[#356](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/issues/356)、[#394](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/394)、[#405](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/405)）。升级的人什么都不用挪：Android 安装包覆盖安装 1.0.3、数据全部保留，桌面端保留设置，1.0.3 的客户端对着新中继（0.25.0）照常工作。

### 亮点

- **iPad 的聊天是一根列，底栏重新是玻璃。** iPad 上，消息、输入框和底栏胶囊共用一根居中的 760 pt 的列，也就是网页控制台的宽度；输入框和底栏彼此一样宽，智能体的回复从 340 pt 的药丸放宽到 600 pt。在每部 iPhone 和 iPad 上，对话现在从输入框和底栏底下滑过去，底栏像在动态和资料库里一样模糊它身后的行，往回翻旧消息时输入框上方升起一层底色的渐变；1.0.3 里底栏垫在一块平底色上、看起来像实心药丸，最后一条消息被输入框的上沿截掉。旁聊的顶栏像主聊天一样化开，想法、目标和资料库站在聊天的底色上，「减弱动态效果」处处生效：底栏的指示块不再弹跳，脸保持不动，欢迎页、抽屉和底栏改为淡入淡出（[#406](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/406)、[#411](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/411)、[#423](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/423)）。
- **你的几台设备意见一致了。** 在 iPhone 上登录会立刻拉下账号的聊天、名字和脸，而不是等它去过一趟后台；在一部没有自己的脸的手机上改名或连接服务，不再把小龙盖到其他每台设备的脸上；另一台设备改的名字和脸会在 App 回到前台时到达；一周以上的聊天又能改名了，标题会跟过去；新设备第一次拉取就加入账号的主聊天，而不是另起一个；模型的隐藏备注不再进入同步的聊天。Android 上，在「设置 › 模型」里选的聊天模型也负责主聊天的第一条消息，在聊天选择器里选的分组成为新聊天的默认并带上分组的思考等级，登录另一个账号从那个账号自己的名字和脸开始（[#408](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/408)、[#409](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/409)、[#410](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/410)、[#421](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/421)）。
- **别的设备上的 Muse 问起某一步，在 iPhone 上回答。** 用 `@〈设备〉` 发出的任务在那台设备的 Muse 上跑；那边有一步需要点头时，以前手机一律拒绝，并在回答下面写一行让你去那台设备上批准。现在手机在输入任务的地方显示卡片，答案和 Android 手机的四个一样：*允许一次*、*在这个聊天里允许*、*在 〈设备〉 上始终允许这一步*（列在智能体页面的*审批*里，可*撤销*）和*拒绝*；付款只有允许一次和拒绝，对方给的时限内没有回答就算拒绝，对方那边先回答了的问题会直接关掉卡片（[#424](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/424)）。
- **DeepSeek Harness 成了第四个编程助手。** 桌面端的「编程助手」页面，以及手机通过 hub 看到的那一页，原来只认识 Cursor、Codex 和 Claude Code；现在 `dsh web` 和 DeepSeek 桌面应用在 `~/.dsh` 下的聊天列在它们旁边，能看记录，能用 `dsh --profile headless --json` 往里发消息，用 `PATH` 上的 `dsh`，没有就用 App 自带的 harness。它的存储是一帧一帧写入的 zstd，每一帧都会被读到。@cutey-none 写的（[#354](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/354)）；Python 运行时暂时还不列出 dsh。
- **不用 key 的网页搜索。** `[connectors.search]` 下的 `provider = "you"`，或者「连接 → 网页搜索」里的 You.com 芯片，不用账号就能搜 You.com，每个地址每天 100 次；超过后结果会说明，DuckDuckGo 代答一次。填上自己的 key 就走额度更高的付费 API，哨兵会显示当前用的是哪个域名。DuckDuckGo 仍是默认。@brainsparker 提议（[#360](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/issues/360)）。
- **密钥放到该放的地方。** 在 App 里填的 MCP 服务器 `env` 值原来以明文写进 `app-settings.json`；现在每个值以 `MCP_<SERVER>_<KEY>` 存进加密的保险库，文件里只留一个引用，网页控制台的 MCP 表单多了「环境变量」一栏并说明这一点。带密码的端点地址会被拒绝，端点跳转时 key 不再发给没有点名的域名，`nanomuse config show` 会打码每一种凭据。Android 的代理密码搬进加密文件，iPhone 的搬进钥匙串，1.0.3 存的密码第一次读取时迁移过去。创建提醒、触发器或目标算作一次改动，`strict` 模式会先问；浏览器把运营商级 NAT 网段和其他私有网段一起拒绝；拉取失败的日历订阅不再显示它的私密链接（[#387](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/387)、[#416](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/416)、[#421](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/421)、[#423](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/423)）。
- **中继的账记得更清楚了。** 邀请奖励在被邀请的朋友第一次使用 nanoMuse Cloud 时发放，而不是注册时，用一次性账号灌水不再有利可图；管理页能看出谁邀请了谁，点出值得看一眼的邀请者并给了一个处理动作；对账号的每个操作者动作都有记录；被停用的账号在发验证码之前就会被告知；删除账号会立刻把它忘掉；退出登录的设备同一刻失去 hub 连接，丢了的手机在这期间够不到账号的其他设备；在两个标签页里打开的网页控制台不再在 hub 上互相顶替；`TRUSTED_PROXIES` 也约束按地址的限流；备份是一致的副本，部署的用户能读（[#384](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/384)、[#391](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/391)、[#401](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/401)、[#413](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/413)）。
- **网页控制台能读出来、能用键盘。** 读屏软件能听到智能体正在写，回复完成后整段读出；菜单打开时焦点落在第一项，方向键移动，Esc 关闭；浏览器视图能用键盘滚动；工具调用的芯片翻译了，选中的那个有标记；动态和技能加载失败时会说明并给出「再试一次」；给 MCP 服务器的工具标 *high* 不再失败，因为芯片改成了运行时认识的 *sensitive*（[#382](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/382)、[#414](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/414)）。
- **Android，哪里坏了修哪里。** Claude 登录不再让 App 退出：provider 用一句话拒绝发送，没有标识的构建不再提供这个登录（[#373](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/issues/373)，@moh8919-dev 报告）。记住付款审批也不再让 App 退出，清单里补上了屏幕锁需要的权限。系统语音输入给回退选对了服务和语言错误（[#393](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/issues/393)，@Mola-maker 在 [#394](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/394) 里修好），取消 provider 语音输入会立刻关掉麦克风（[#356](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/issues/356)），自己的视频超过 30 MB 就停止复制，带引号的句子又显示引号了，任务进行中断线不再让 hub 把 App 带崩，别的设备交来的任务用那台设备的语言回答，底栏在 TalkBack 下读作标签页，聊天的导航区域和输入框的圆角渐变融在一起（[#397](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/397)，@Ichiro786），`./gradlew :app:lintDebug` 又能跑了。
- **iPhone，哪里坏了修哪里。** ••• 菜单的「新对话」又能打开对话了（[#392](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/issues/392)，@bldr3 报告）；密码错了会说清楚，九种语言都有；「服务商」下的 nanoMuse Cloud 一行不再说「额度剩余 0%」；中继的 key 从第一刻起只写进这部手机的钥匙串；自己的视频不进备份，所有 H.264 文件都接受；例行任务的说明写的是手机真正做的事；三处翻译的数字顺序改回来了。
- **桌面端跟上了。** 失败的更新检查一小时内会再试；自己的视频在这台电脑能播的情况下可以是 HEVC，规则和其他每台设备一样；分片的 MP4 能正确读取；一次发布会拿到每个构建成功的平台的安装包，工作流还会检查聊天样式表依赖的每个钩子在待发布的 dsh 版本里都存在（[#383](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/383)、[#415](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/415)）。
- **展示站和项目本身。** 托管的展示站只相信来自 Caddy 的 `X-Forwarded-For`，只允许项目自己的站点嵌入它，启动失败的演示不算一次使用，自己的页面也说中文，手机从钉死的一个 MobileGym 提交构建，带健康检查，隐私页写明它保存什么（[#381](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/381)、[#417](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/417)）。每个 GitHub Action 都钉到提交，桌面端的检查在每个 PR 上也跑 Windows，发布脚本拒绝做到一半的发布，issue 表单中英双语，文档经过两种语言的审读后与代码一致，README 的访客计数器换到了 hits.sh（[#378](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/378)、[#380](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/380)、[#390](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/390)、[#412](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/412)、[#418](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/pull/418)）。
- **TestFlight。** 第 18 个（1.0.1）和第 19 个构建（1.0.2）在 2026-10-12 通过了 Apple 的测试版审核，所以公开链接今天装到的是第 19 个构建；第 22 个构建（1.0.3）还在审核中；1.0.4 以第 26 个构建上传并提交同一审核。审核通过的构建就会给到链接，内部测试员在那之前就能用。

### 升级说明

- **Android 覆盖安装 1.0.3，数据保留。** `nanoMuse-1.0.4-arm64.apk`（Android 8.0+，arm64；versionCode 47）和之前每个版本用同一把签名。校验：`sha256sum -c nanoMuse-1.0.4-arm64.apk.sha256`。如果设过代理密码，第一次读取时会搬进加密文件；其他什么都没挪。
- **iPhone 和 iPad 通过 TestFlight。** 公开链接装到的是第 19 个构建（1.0.2），它和第 18 个一起通过了 Apple 的测试版审核；第 22 个构建（1.0.3）还在审核中，第 26 个构建（1.0.4）上传后送审；哪个构建审核通过，链接就给到哪个，内部测试员在那之前就能用。如果设过代理密码，第一次读取时会搬进钥匙串。
- **桌面安装包由 CI 从 tag `v1.0.4` 构建。** Mac 的包没有经过公证：第一次在「系统设置 → 隐私与安全性」里点「仍要打开」（或右键 → 「打开」）。Windows 的包没有签名：点一次「更多信息」和「仍要运行」。Linux 上优先用 `.deb`；AppImage 需要 FUSE（没有就加 `--appimage-extract-and-run`）。它们比 APK 晚一点到，`SHA256SUMS-desktop.txt` 列出了所有校验值。你的设置留在原处；`~/.dsh` 下有 dsh 聊天的电脑会在「设置 → 编程助手」里看到它们。
- **中继是 0.25.0，1.0.3 的客户端对着它照常工作。** 给运维的人：邀请奖励现在在被邀请账号第一次计费请求时发放，这条规则之前邀请的账号会在中继第一次带着它启动时标记为已发；来自非常见邮箱域名的新账号每滚动 24 小时限 `SIGNUP_PER_DOMAIN_DAY` 个（默认 20，0 为关闭；`SIGNUP_DOMAIN_ALLOW` 加上你自己的域名）；`TRUSTED_PROXIES` 现在也管按地址的限流；`GET /v1/config` 带上了 `code_ttl_s` 和 `password_min_len`；被吊销的 key 会让它的 hub 连接以 `4001 bad_key` 关闭；备份副本属于中继目录的所有者（或 `BACKUP_OWNER`），权限 600。细节见 [docs/self-hosting.md](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/blob/main/docs/zh/self-hosting.md) 和 [cloud/README.md](https://github.com/nano-muse/nanoMuse/blob/main/cloud/README.md)。
- **运行时的设置文件要么整个写好，要么不写。** `app-settings.json` 先写在旧文件旁边再改名覆盖；MCP 服务器的 `env` 值在下次从 App 里添加该服务器时搬进保险库，`config.toml` 里的明文值仍然可用。`config.toml` 里的每个键都保持原样；`[connectors.search] provider = "you"` 是新增的，可选。
